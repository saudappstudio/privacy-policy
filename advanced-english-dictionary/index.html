This Privacy Policy applies to the Advanced English Dictionary mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are strictly committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("user" or "you").

1. Overview & Data Safety Commitment

We designed Advanced English Dictionary to serve as a comprehensive offline and online vocabulary tool, translation suite, voice speech assistant, and learning companion. We strictly comply with Google Play Developer Policies, Google Play Data Safety declarations, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information We Collect & Data Storage

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide fast offline functionality and respect user privacy, the following features store data 100% locally on your device using Android Room Database and DataStore/Preferences. This data is never transmitted, collected, or uploaded to our servers:

  • Search History & Recent Lookups: Local records of searched words, phrases, idioms, and translations.
  • Favorites & Saved Bookmarks: Custom saved lists, bookmarked vocabulary, expressions, grammar rules, conversations, collocations, proverbs, and phrasal verbs.
  • App Preferences: Display themes (Light/Dark), font scale, Text-To-Speech pitch/rate, and notification preferences.

B. Android System Permissions & Hardware Access

To deliver advanced learning features, the App requests specific hardware and system permissions. These are strictly used for their intended functional purposes:

  • Microphone / Audio (RECORD_AUDIO): Used for Voice Search and Voice Translation (Speech-to-Text). Speech input is processed in real-time to convert spoken audio into text. We do not record, save, store, or transmit your audio recordings to our servers.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ to deliver optional learning notifications such as "Word of the Day", study reminders, and app feature announcements.
  • Foreground Service & Data Sync (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): Enables WorkManager to securely download Google ML Kit translation language models in the background with persistent foreground notifications.
  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to download ML Kit translation models, perform online dictionary/translation lookups, deliver push notifications, log stability crash reports, and display ads.

C. Technical & Diagnostic Information Collected Automatically

When using the App, certain non-personally identifiable technical information is collected automatically by integrated third-party SDKs to maintain performance, stability, and app features:

  • Device Metadata: Device model, manufacturer, Android OS version, system language, and hardware specifications.
  • Usage & Crash Analytics: Anonymous app session events via Firebase Analytics, as well as anonymous stack traces, memory statistics, crash logs, and app state at the time of an error via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Notification Messaging Data: Anonymous Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) installation tokens and Firebase In-App Messaging (FIAM) event triggers.
  • Online Query Processing: When performing online word definitions, synonym lookups, or web translations, non-personal search query strings are transmitted securely over HTTPS to public APIs (Free Dictionary API, Datamuse API, and Google Translate API). No personal identifiers are attached to these requests.

3. Advertising Policy & GDPR Consent (Google AdMob)

To support the ongoing development of this App as a free tool, we integrate third-party advertising via Google AdMob and manage privacy choices using Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

  • Ad Delivery & Device Identifiers: AdMob may collect Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and device metadata to serve relevant advertisements.
  • GDPR & User Consent Choices: For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, consent choices regarding personalized vs non-personalized ads are gathered at app launch via Google UMP consent forms.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized interest-based ads on Android via Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Data Retention & User Erasure Rights

Since we do not create or manage online user accounts, all your saved data (history, bookmarks, notes, favorites) remains stored locally on your physical device. You can immediately delete 100% of your stored data at any time by:

  • Navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Advanced English Dictionary > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App from your device.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is designed for general audiences and language learners. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete such information.
