This Privacy Policy applies to the Advanced English Dictionary mobile application (hereinafter referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("user" or "you").

1. Overview & Commitment

We designed Advanced English Dictionary to provide dictionary lookup, voice translation, vocabulary learning, and text recognition services. We comply strictly with Google Play Developer Policies, including the Google Play Data Safety requirements, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Search Queries & Favorites: Words, idioms, and phrases you search for or save as bookmarks are stored locally on your device for your convenience and offline functionality.
  • User Feedback / Support Requests: If you contact us via email, we collect your email address and any information provided in your correspondence to assist you.

B. Device & Technical Information Collected Automatically

When you access and use the App, certain information is collected automatically by integrated third-party SDKs for performance, analytics, and advertising purposes:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID), Device Model, Operating System Version, Hardware Specs.
  • Network & Usage Data: IP Address, Mobile Network Information, App Launch Times, Screen Views, Feature Usage, and Crash Logs.
  • Cookies & Web Beacons: Identifiers used by advertising partners to deliver tailored advertisements.

C. App Permissions & Data Usage

To deliver advanced dictionary and translation features, the App may request access to specific hardware features. These permissions are used exclusively for their stated purposes:

  • Microphone / Audio (Voice Search & Translation): Allows you to speak words or sentences for translation and pronunciation. Audio input is processed locally or transmitted via encrypted channels to speech recognition engines. We do not record, store, or sell audio files.
  • Camera (Camera Search / Text Recognition / OCR): Allows you to scan printed text for dictionary lookups or translation. Images are processed in real-time on your device to extract text. We do not store, capture, or transmit your camera photos to external servers.
  • Internet & Network State: Required to fetch online definitions, translation results, dictionary updates, and load advertisements.

3. Advertising Policy (Google AdMob)

To support the development and maintenance of this App as a free service, we integrate third-party advertising services, primarily Google AdMob.

  • Personalized & Non-Personalized Ads: AdMob may collect and use your Android Advertising ID, IP address, and device metadata to display relevant advertisements.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based advertising on your Android device by navigating to:
    Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Reset Advertising ID).

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Locally stored data (such as bookmarks and history) remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Services are not intended for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. User Data Rights & Data Deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as GDPR in the EU or CCPA in California), you have rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Portability: You have the right to request information about the data collected about you.
  • Data Deletion Request: Since we do not maintain user accounts on an external server, most data is stored locally on your device. You can erase all app data instantly by going to Android Settings > Apps > Advanced English Dictionary > Storage > Clear Data or by uninstalling the App.
  • Contact for Data Requests: If you wish to submit a data inquiry or deletion request regarding third-party services, please contact us at saud.appstudio@gmail.com.
